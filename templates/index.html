Diabetes Risk

5 basic inputs needed

Diabetes
Fasting blood sugar — normal is 70–100 mg/dL
Body Mass Index — normal is 18.5–24.9
Family history score — 0 (none) to 2.5 (strong history)
Enter 0 if male or never pregnant

Kidney Disease Risk

8 basic inputs needed

CKD
Diastolic BP — normal is 60–80 mm Hg
Urine albumin level — 0 is normal, 5 is very high
Normal: 12–17 g/dL. Low = possible anemia
Normal: 0.6–1.2 mg/dL. High = kidney stress

⚠️ This tool is for early screening only — not a medical diagnosis. Always consult a doctor.
